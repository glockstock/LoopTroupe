## 2026-10-02 — owner

Verbatim:

> "We used the main gate. Our must do rides were Steel Vengence, Top Thrill 2, Siren's Curse, Millenium Force, and Maverick. We also enjoyed Gatekeeper and Valravyn. We went straight to Steel Vengence to start since it was at the back of the park and had the lowest guest volume. There's probably a better gate for that but we didn't use it this time. If Steel Vengence is down, just backtrack a bit and hit Millenium Force. Use fast pass to do the top rides, especially Top Thrill 2, Steel Vengence, and Siren's Curse."

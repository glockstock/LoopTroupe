# Open Questions

Any agent may add, clarify, or update questions here; keep IDs stable. Recording a proposal does not approve it. Approved product answers go to `docs/spec.md` (via `product_manager`), approved technical answers to `docs/tech_spec.md` (via `engineering_manager`).

| ID | Question | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q-001 | How should accounts and cloud-saved credits work (sign-in method, migration of existing browser logs, hosting/backend)? | Open: direction approved, approach pending | Owner approved accounts on 2026-10-02 ("You log in and log your rides"). `engineering_manager` is writing options in `docs/proposals/accounts-and-payments.md`; a big change per AGENTS.md, so the owner picks the approach. Product requirements: no lost credits on migration, full export, account deletion, logging no slower than today (spec Phase 2). |
| Q-002 | How are relocated, renamed, or re-themed coasters counted? Is a relocated coaster one credit or two? | Open | Enthusiast conventions vary; needs a product ruling before `coaster_data_curator` encodes it. Matters more once credits are public and comparable. |
| Q-003 | Should the database expand beyond the United States? | Open | The owner said "every park in America to start." Guides also start with US parks. |
| Q-004 | How is the database kept current each season, and from which sources? | Open | The 2024 scrape and build script were not kept; edits are manual. More urgent now: guides depend on an accurate ride list for their parks. |
| Q-005 | Should web fonts be self-hosted rather than loaded from Google Fonts? | Open | Reliability, privacy, and layout-shift tradeoffs; owned by `web_performance_engineer`. |
| Q-006 | Does the owner approve the proposed roadmap order: (1) one-guide demand test, (2) accounts and community credits, (3) tips plus more guides, (4) generated park maps, (5) ride zoom-in? | Open | Owner asked to see the plan before committing. PM recommends this order, with Phases 1 and 2 allowed to overlap. Main alternative: build accounts first and launch guides on top; slower to learn whether guides sell. |
| Q-007 | What is the guide pricing model and price? | Open | Options: per-park one-time purchase; annual all-guides pass; trip pass; subscription. PM recommends a one-time per-park guide at about $9 for the Phase 1 test, and revisiting an annual pass once 3+ guides exist. Reference: TouringPlans about $10–25/yr per destination; Ride Ready $24.99 per 14 days. |
| Q-008 | Which park gets the first guide, and which come next? | Open | PM recommends Cedar Point first. Next candidates: Kings Island, Dollywood, Six Flags Magic Mountain, Knoebels, Carowinds. Criteria: enthusiast demand, coaster count, logistics complexity, owner's ability to verify first-hand. |
| Q-009 | What format is a guide, and how much is free? | Open | PM recommends a mobile-first web guide inside Loop Troupe (not a PDF), usable with weak signal, with at-a-glance, a must-ride teaser, and video picks free. Owner to confirm. |
| Q-010 | For the Phase 1 test, may we use a third-party checkout and collect aggregate visit and purchase counts? | Open | AGENTS.md forbids analytics or third-party services without owner approval. PM recommends allowing aggregate-only counts (no ride data, no cross-site tracking) and the simplest checkout `engineering_manager` proposes. Without measurement, the test cannot pass or fail. |
| Q-011 | Structured tips or a forum for community input, and how is it moderated? | Open | Owner suggested "some kind of community forum." PM recommends structured, dated, voted tips attached to a park, ride, gate, or food spot (forums already exist; tips feed guides directly). Moderation to decide: sign-in required to post, report button, review queue at launch, who moderates. Should tips be free to read? PM recommends yes. |
| Q-012 | What style should the ride zoom-in take, and which ride stats do we show? | Open | Options: (a) pixel-isometric animated vignette (fits the approved look), (b) stylized low-poly 3D in the retro palette, (c) realistic 3D (departs from the approved look). PM recommends (a), with (b) as a later experiment; consult `art_director`. Stats: height, speed, length where an open source allows; G-force only where published; no copying from copyrighted databases. |
| Q-013 | Who researches and verifies guides, and is a first-hand visit required before charging? | Open | PM recommends AI-assisted research plus human verification of every claim, and at least one first-hand visit (owner or trusted contributor) to each paid-guide park per season. Owner to say whether that is realistic. |
| Q-014 | Should the free side ever carry ads, or rider data ever be sold or shared? | Open | PM recommends no ads and no data sales; guides fund the product. |
| Q-015 | Are profiles, credits, and reviews public by default once accounts exist? | Open | PM recommends asking each rider at sign-up with a clear choice (public profile or private), defaulting to private for migrated local logs. Community value vs. the project's privacy-first history. |
| Q-016 | Does "app" mean app-store apps, or is the mobile website enough? | Open | PM recommends staying on the mobile web through Phase 3, then revisiting. App stores add review, fees on digital purchases, and maintenance. |
| Q-017 | Does Neon's managed auth work from a plain-JS page with no bundler and without third-party cookies (including Safari)? | Open | Gates the Neon recommendation in `docs/proposals/accounts-and-payments.md`; a 1–2 day spike decides. Fallback: Supabase. Raised by `engineering_manager`. |
